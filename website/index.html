System Architecture

How It Works

Four interconnected stages transform raw optical camera photons into millisecond desktop control.

1
📷

Webcam Capture

OpenCV accesses any standard USB or built-in webcam at 30-60 FPS, applies horizontal mirroring, and normalizes frames.

2
🧠

MediaPipe Tracking

Neural networks identify the hand skeleton and predict 21 spatial landmarks with 3D depth and handedness confidence.

3
⚡

Gesture Engine

Geometric classification calculates joint angles, finger extension, euclidean distances, and velocity for debounced gestures.

4
🎯

Action Execution

PyAutoGUI moves the OS cursor and executes clicks, while Pygame renders blade trails and slices flying arcade fruits.

Project Background

Accessible Human-Computer Interaction

GestureMouse AI was created to explore the future of touchless interaction. By combining cutting-edge computer vision with lightweight edge neural networks, it provides an intuitive, hands-free way to navigate desktops without expensive specialized hardware.

Designed for accessibility, hygienic interaction in sterile environments, presentation control, and casual gaming, it proves that any standard webcam can become an interactive optical sensor.
